Read CLAUDE.md ("Current Status" checklist) and docs/05-schedule-12-weeks.md.

1. Identify the current incomplete phase.
2. Summarize its deliverables and what already exists in the repo.
3. Propose the next 2–3 concrete tasks (small, shippable, deployable).
4. On my confirmation, implement them following the Hard Rules in CLAUDE.md.
5. After completing work: update the CLAUDE.md status checklist, ensure tests/evals pass, and remind me to deploy.
