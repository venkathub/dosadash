# Serving Hours Guide

DosaDash runs like a real Tamil tiffin centre crossed with a highway mess:
different counters fire at different times of day, every day (all times
IST). The app and the ordering assistant always show live availability —
if a dish can be added to your cart, it is being served right now.

## When is each counter open?

| Counter | Dishes | Hours (daily) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiffin griddle | all dosas, idlis, vadas, uttapams, millet dosa/idli, kuzhi paniyaram, idiyappam, appam, adai, kitchadi | 6:00–11:30 AM and 5:00–10:00 PM |
| Pongal pots | Ven Pongal, Sweet Pongal, Thinai Pongal | 6:00 AM–12:00 noon only |
| Mini Tiffin plate | Mini Tiffin | breakfast only, 6:00–11:30 AM |
| Rice & biryani | curd/lemon/tamarind/sambar rice, all biryanis | 11:30 AM–10:00 PM |
| Curry & mess pots | Chettinad curries, Mess Specials (mutton chukka, nattu kozhi, karuvadu, nandu, prawn) | 11:30 AM–10:00 PM |
| Veg meals | South Indian Veg Meals | two sittings: 11:30 AM–3:30 PM and 7:00–10:30 PM |
| Non-veg mess meals | Non-Veg Mess Meals | lunch only, 11:30 AM–4:00 PM |
| Snack fryer | Aloo Bonda, Onion Bajji | 3:00–9:00 PM |
| Parotta counter | Parotta, Kothu Parotta | 4:00–11:00 PM |
| Sweets & beverages | all sweets, filter coffee, chai, neer mor, rose milk, sukku coffee | all day |

## Why can't I order a dosa at lunch?

Like a traditional tiffin centre, the dosa griddle rests between 11:30 AM
and 5:00 PM while the kitchen turns to rice plates, biryani, and mess
cooking. Dosas and other tiffin dishes are back for the evening rush from
5 PM. The same rhythm applies in reverse: biryani and curries start only at
11:30 AM.

## Notes

- Hours are the same all seven days.
- A dish can also be temporarily sold out inside its window — the menu
  shows live availability either way.
- Business hours for the kitchen as a whole are separate and shown in the
  app footer.
