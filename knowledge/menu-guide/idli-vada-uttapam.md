# Idli, Vada & Uttapam Guide

Steamed and griddled tiffin classics. Idlis are steamed fresh through the
day; vadas are fried to order. All are served with sambar and chutneys.

Everything on this page runs on tiffin-centre hours: breakfast 6:00–11:30 AM
IST and dinner 5:00–10:00 PM IST, every day. Like a traditional tiffin
centre, the steamers and fryers rest at lunch (11:30 AM–5 PM), when the
kitchen switches to rice plates and mess cooking.

## Idli (2 pcs) — ₹80

Two steamed, fluffy fermented rice-lentil cakes with sambar and two
chutneys. Naturally vegan, Jain-friendly, and gluten-free — the gentlest
item on the menu and a go-to for anyone unwell or ordering for kids.
Fastest item in the kitchen (about 10 minutes).
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Mini Idli Sambar — ₹100

Fourteen button idlis soaked in hot sambar tempered with ghee. Contains
dairy (ghee). Comfort food; ask for "ghee on the side" to make it vegan.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Podi Idli — ₹110

Idlis quartered and tossed in spicy gunpowder podi with oil. Medium heat.
Vegan. Travels very well — no gravy to spill.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Medu Vada (2 pcs) — ₹90

Golden, crisp urad-dal doughnuts with black pepper, onion, and curry
leaves — crunchy outside, soft inside. Vegan. Fried to order in about 12
minutes. Add one to any tiffin order; the classic combo is "idli-vada".
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Curd Vada — ₹110

Medu vadas soaked in whipped, seasoned curd with a mustard-coriander
tempering. Contains dairy (curd) and mustard. Jain-friendly. Served chilled
— a cooling counter to spicy mains.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Rava Idli — ₹100

Karnataka-style steamed semolina idli with curd, cashew, and a mustard
tempering. Contains gluten (semolina), dairy (curd), tree nut (cashew), and
mustard — the most allergen-dense tiffin item, so flag allergies before
ordering. Jain-friendly.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Onion Uttapam — ₹120

Thick griddle cake from the dosa batter, topped with onions and green
chilli. Vegan. Softer and more filling than a dosa.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Tomato Onion Uttapam — ₹130

Uttapam with a tangy tomato-onion topping. Vegan. Mild.
Serving window: 6–11:30 AM and 5–10 PM IST (not served at lunch).

## Pairing notes

- Classic breakfast: Idli (2 pcs) + Medu Vada + Filter Coffee.
- Lightest meal: Idli or Set Dosa with extra sambar.
- Gluten-free: everything here EXCEPT Rava Idli.
- Vegan: Idli, Podi Idli, Medu Vada, and both uttapams.
- Millet route: the Kambu Idli (2 pcs) in the Millet Specials guide is the
  pearl-millet cousin of the classic idli, on the same serving windows.
