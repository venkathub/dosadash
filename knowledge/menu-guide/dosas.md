# Dosa Guide

Every dosa is made to order from batter fermented in-house overnight (rice +
urad dal, stone-ground). All dosas are served with coconut chutney, tomato
chutney, and sambar unless noted. Typical prep time is 15–20 minutes.

## Plain Dosa — ₹90

The classic: thin, crisp, golden. Fermented rice-lentil batter, nothing else.
Vegan and Jain-friendly. The safest first order for kids and mild palates.
Pairs well with an extra sambar or a Filter Coffee.

## Masala Dosa — ₹120

Crisp dosa wrapped around a spiced potato-onion masala tempered with mustard
seeds and curry leaves. Our single most-ordered dish. Mildly spiced; the
masala can be packed separately on request so the dosa stays crisp on
delivery.

## Mysore Masala Dosa — ₹140

Same potato filling as the Masala Dosa, but the inside of the dosa is smeared
with a fiery red chutney of dried red chillies and chana dal before folding.
Medium heat — noticeably spicier than the Masala Dosa. Ask for "less spicy"
and the kitchen goes lighter on the red chutney.

## Ghee Roast Dosa — ₹150

Roasted slowly in generous ghee until deep golden and extra crisp, usually
served as a tall cone. Jain-friendly (no onion or garlic) but NOT vegan —
it contains dairy (ghee). Best eaten immediately; among dosas it travels
the least well over long delivery distances.

## Podi Dosa — ₹110

Dosa dusted inside with "gunpowder" idli podi (roasted lentil-chilli powder)
and gingelly oil. Medium heat with a nutty, smoky flavour. Vegan.

## Onion Dosa — ₹100

Dosa layered with caramelised onions and green chillies pressed into the
batter side. Mild, sweet-savoury. Vegan.

## Rava Dosa — ₹130

Lacy, instant crepe made from semolina (rava) with cumin, black pepper, and
curry leaves — no fermentation, so the texture is netted and crisp rather
than spongy. Contains gluten (semolina). Jain-friendly. Takes about 20
minutes; it cannot be rushed without tearing.

## Set Dosa — ₹110

A set of three small, thick, spongy dosas — soft rather than crisp, closer
to a pancake. Served with coconut chutney. Jain-friendly and vegan. Good
choice for anyone who finds crisp dosas hard to manage (elderly, toddlers).

## Cheese Dosa — ₹150

Crisp dosa with molten cheese and a brush of butter — the kids' favourite.
Contains dairy (cheese, butter). Jain-friendly. Mild.

## Kal Dosa — ₹100

Thick, soft, street-style griddle dosa — chewy edges, spongy centre. Vegan
and Jain-friendly. Traditionally paired with a spicy curry: try it with
Kara Kuzhambu or Chicken Chettinad.

## Pairing notes

- Spice-averse: Plain Dosa, Set Dosa, Cheese Dosa.
- Spice-seeking: Mysore Masala Dosa, Podi Dosa + Kara Kuzhambu on the side.
- Vegan orders: any dosa EXCEPT Ghee Roast Dosa and Cheese Dosa.
- Gluten-free: every dosa EXCEPT Rava Dosa (all others are rice-lentil).
