# Millet Specials & Mess Specials

Two new counters, inspired by the highway legends of NH-45 — the
Chennai–Trichy road every Tamil food lover knows by its pit stops. The
Millet Specials channel 99 KM Coffee, the pure-veg traditional-millet
kitchen at Perumbairkandigai, where ragi dosas come off an open griddle and
sukku coffee steams in the morning fog. The Mess Specials channel Manna
Mess at Acharapakkam, the famous non-veg mess where lorry drivers and
weekend road-trippers queue side by side for mutton chukka and karuvadu.
The menu always shows exactly what is being served right now.

## Millet Specials

Pure-veg, traditional-grain tiffin in the 99 KM Coffee style. Millets
(ragi, kambu, thinai) are naturally gluten-free and gentler on the stomach
than polished rice — the health-forward corner of the menu.

### Ragi Millet Dosa — ₹110

Earthy finger-millet dosa off the open griddle, 99-KM highway style.
Deep-brown, nutty, and crisp at the edges. Vegan, Jain-friendly, and
gluten-free. Mild. Pairs with kara chutney or a Chettinad curry at dinner.

### Kambu Idli (2 pcs) — ₹90

Soft pearl-millet (kambu) idlis served with kara chutney — the rustic,
darker cousin of the classic idli. Vegan, Jain-friendly, gluten-free, mild.

### Thinai Pongal — ₹120

Foxtail-millet (thinai) pongal with cracked black pepper, ghee, and
cashews — Ven Pongal's old-grain ancestor. Contains dairy (ghee) and tree
nut (cashew). Jain-friendly. Like all pongals, it is a morning dish.
Serving window: mornings only, 6 AM–12 noon IST.

### Kuzhi Paniyaram (7 pcs) — ₹100

Seven griddle-pit dumplings from fermented batter with onion and green
chilli — crisp outside, steamy inside. Vegan, mild. A breakfast or
evening-tiffin snack that travels beautifully.

## Mess Specials

Non-veg mess cooking in the Manna Mess tradition: kal-chatti (stone-pot)
gravies, cracked pepper, and no shortcuts. These are the boldest dishes on
the menu — most sit at spice level 3, hotter than the biryanis. All five
serve 11:30 AM–10 PM IST (lunch and dinner). Pair with plain rice, Parotta
(from 4 PM), or Kal Dosa in the evening window.

### Mutton Chukka — ₹280

Dry-tossed mutton chukka, kal-chatti style — the Manna Mess classic.
Cracked pepper, curry leaves, and slow-roasted onion. Non-veg, hot (spice
level 3). A dry dish: great as a side to Sambar Rice or the Non-Veg Mess
Meals.

### Nattu Kozhi Kuzhambu — ₹260

Country-chicken (nattu kozhi) curry simmered in a ground village masala
with coconut. Non-veg, hot (spice level 3). Deeper and more rustic than
Chicken Chettinad — the gravy to flood a plate of rice with.

### Karuvadu Thokku — ₹220

Dry-fish (karuvadu) thokku, tangy and fierce — a highway-mess signature
built on onion, tomato, tamarind, and dried red chilli. Non-veg, hot
(spice level 3). ALLERGY NOTE: contains fish (karuvadu is sun-dried fish) —
avoid with any fish or seafood allergy. An acquired, beloved taste; a
little goes a long way over hot rice.

### Nandu Pepper Fry — ₹300

Crab (nandu) tossed in cracked black pepper and curry leaves — the
priciest and most theatrical dish on the menu. Non-veg, hot (spice level
3). ALLERGY NOTE: contains shellfish (crab) — avoid with any shellfish
allergy. Eat-with-your-hands food; best ordered fresh, not for long
delivery distances.

### Prawn Thokku — ₹280

Juicy prawns in a thick onion-tomato masala with Chettinad spice. Non-veg,
medium heat (spice level 2) — the mildest of the mess specials. ALLERGY
NOTE: contains shellfish (prawn) — avoid with any shellfish allergy.
Excellent with Parotta or plain rice.

## Pairing notes

- Highway breakfast, 99-KM style: Ragi Millet Dosa or Kambu Idli + Sukku Coffee
  (dry-ginger jaggery brew, all day — see the Beverages guide).
- Gluten-free and vegan: Ragi Millet Dosa, Kambu Idli, Kuzhi Paniyaram.
- Manna Mess lunch: Non-Veg Mess Meals (lunch only, see the Tiffin & Meals
  guide) + Mutton Chukka on the side.
- Seafood allergies: Karuvadu Thokku contains fish; Nandu Pepper Fry and
  Prawn Thokku contain shellfish. Everything in the Millet Specials section
  is fully vegetarian — the two counters share nothing but the highway.
