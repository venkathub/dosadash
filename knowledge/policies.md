# Ordering, Cancellation & Refund Policies

These are the operative rules the platform enforces. The AI assistant must
answer policy questions from this document and must not invent exceptions.

## Cancellation policy

- You may cancel an order yourself **only while it is in PLACED status** —
  i.e. before the kitchen confirms it. Use the order page or ask support.
- Once an order is CONFIRMED or COOKING, self-service cancellation is no
  longer possible (ingredients are committed). Contact support; staff can
  cancel at their discretion before the order is READY.
- Orders that are READY, OUT FOR DELIVERY, or DELIVERED cannot be cancelled.

## Refund policy

- Cancelled paid orders are refunded in full to the original payment method.
- Refunds are issued by restaurant staff after review; typical processing
  time is 5–7 business days depending on your bank (instant in this demo's
  Razorpay TEST mode).
- Quality issues (wrong item, spoiled or badly damaged food): report within
  24 hours of delivery with a photo; support can issue a full or partial
  refund after review.
- Delivery issues (order never arrived): full refund after verification.
- Refunds are always to the original payment method — no cash refunds, no
  store credit at this time.

## Order changes

- After an order is placed, items cannot be added or removed by the
  customer. Support staff can modify an order before it starts COOKING.
- For additions, the fastest path is a second order.

## Delivery policy

- We deliver only to the pincodes listed at checkout (Chennai:
  600001, 600002, 600004, 600017 in the demo configuration). Addresses
  outside these areas cannot complete checkout.
- If a delivery fails because the address or phone number provided was
  unreachable, the order is not refundable.
- Contactless delivery: request it in the order notes.

## Hours & kitchen availability

- Orders are accepted only within the kitchen's configured business hours;
  the restaurant may also pause the kitchen temporarily (rush, maintenance).
  While closed or paused, no channel — web, Telegram, or AI assistant — will
  accept an order.
- Individual dishes may have their own serving windows (e.g. breakfast-only
  items) and may sell out during the day.

## Allergy disclaimer

- Allergen information per dish is published in the Allergen & Dietary
  Guide and is generated directly from the kitchen's recipe data.
- The kitchen uses shared equipment and a shared fryer: trace cross-contact
  between dishes is possible. Dishes are NOT guaranteed allergen-free.
- If you have a severe allergy, state it in the order notes. The assistant
  will warn you when a dish conflicts with allergens saved in your profile,
  but the final responsibility for checking rests with the customer.

## Pricing & tax

- Menu prices are in INR and exclude GST; 5% GST (food rate) is added at
  checkout and shown on the bill.
- Prices may change; the price at the time of checkout is the one charged.

## Demo deployment notice

This is a portfolio demonstration. Payments run exclusively in Razorpay
TEST mode: no real charges, no real deliveries, and synthetic order history
is used for analytics and forecasting demos.
